THE EXACT SINGLETON, WITHOUT ARTIFICIAL DRIVERS
Read a row from its right edge. Let three transformations of binary streams be
  A=(A,C), B=swap(A,C), C=swap(B,C).
The notation specifies the next state on input 0/1 and whether to flip that
input bit. In detail, the transition/output table is
  A: 0->0,A; 1->1,C
  B: 0->1,A; 1->0,C
  C: 0->1,B; 1->0,C.
The state transition reads the INPUT bit, not the emitted bit.

Let e=1000... . The official Rule 30 singleton row at age t, in right-edge
coordinates, is exactly A^t(e). Hence for t>=1 its center is coordinate t-1
of C^t(000...). This moving coordinate is essential.

A dual operation d_b acts on words of A,B,C: scan the word left to right,
replace each symbol by its next state under the current bit b, and update b
to its emitted bit. Let par(w) be the parity of the number of B/C symbols.
With d_0 repeated n times,
  center(n+1) = par(d_0^n(C^(n+1))).
SeedAutomaton.center_dual proves this for EVERY n using the official cell and
center definitions. It is a reformulation, not a finite-prefix fit.

THE ACTUAL SECTIONS AND A SHARP POSITIVE-WORD LENGTH
Set w_n=d_0^n(C^(n+1)). SeedSections.core_row proves the stronger identity
  w_n(000...)(k) = cell(n+1,-k) for EVERY n,k.
Thus these words carry the entire actual center-to-left half-row at a fixed
physical origin, including its black left edge at k=n+1.

Every two dual rounds produce an erasable leading A. An A prefix acts
trivially on the particular input 000..., although A is NOT the identity
transformation on arbitrary inputs. Erasing floor(n/2) leading A symbols
represents the same zero-input output with exactly floor((n+3)/2) symbols.

This length is minimal: a positive word of L symbols can only make black
cells at positions <2L when acting on 000.... The actual half-row is black
at position n+1, so n+1<2L. Upper and lower bounds meet.

SectionCompression.exact_minimum_positive_length proves, for every age t>=1,
that the minimum is ceil((t+1)/2). The model is strictly POSITIVE words in
the three specified transformations, representing the WHOLE half-row on
the zero input. It is NOT a general algorithmic memory/time lower bound,
not a result about words allowing inverses, and not Prize Problem 3.

WHAT THE GROUP CONNECTION ACTUALLY SAYS
The machine is number 5002 in the encoding of Bondarenko et al.,
"Classification of groups generated by 3-state automata over a 2-letter
alphabet", arXiv:0803.3555. Renaming A->c, B->b, C->a gives their number
2369 exactly. Their table and discussion identify the group as noncontracting.
Sources: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0803.3555, sections 6,7 and the discussion of
2369 in section 9. The finite numbering/renaming was independently checked.
The external group's properties are not imported as Lean axioms.

An elementary obstruction was also proved internally: every A^m survives
as its own section along arbitrarily long all-zero prefixes, and those
functions are distinct for different m. More relevantly, even the ACTUAL
selected words w_n have different zero-input outputs and no uniformly bounded
positive-word representatives. Thus a fixed lossless catalogue of these
sections cannot supply the desired proof. This does not rule out a suitable
quotient that retains only information needed to prove parity aperiodicity.

THE MISSING STEP
The one-time compression does not yield a closed recurrence on a fixed
family of compressed words. It leaves a growing residual word, and its
single parity bit may lose information carried by the full word. Infinite
group order, noncontractivity, and unbounded representation length do not
by themselves force that parity to be nonperiodic.

A useful next research proposal would need an explicit recurrence for the
compressed singleton family and an argument about its parity, preserving
the relation between n and the moving readout index. Neither a closed such
recurrence nor an aperiodicity argument was found in this block. Merely
renaming the parity nonperiodicity statement is not a smaller solved lemma.
This route is an audited alternative encoding, not evidence that the main
proof gap has narrowed.
