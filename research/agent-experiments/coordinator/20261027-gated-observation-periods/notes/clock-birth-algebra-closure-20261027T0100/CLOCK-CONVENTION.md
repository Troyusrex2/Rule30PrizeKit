# Clock convention for the oldest-defect paragraph

The algebra/module classifications in PAPER.md hold for any supplied dyadic
whole period N. To APPLY its oldest nonreturning-coordinate paragraph to a
counterexample, choose N to be the LEAST whole-prefix period, with N>2.
Equivalently, assume explicitly that the whole prefix fails return at N/2.
Then the oldest such coordinate exists and its half-period defect is one.
An oversized supplied period can have no such coordinate; no birth is
asserted in that case. No individual-column period replaces this whole
clock. The original paper and first manifest remain byte-unchanged.
