# What full temporal algebraic closure retains at a clock birth

Paper-only route audit. No computation, sweep, new finite certificate or Lean
build. This closes a particular proposed closed-state representation for K;
it does not prove or disprove the finite-white K=0 exclusion.

## Actual target and proposed spatial filtration

Consider one genuine finite-white diagonal orbit with whole period N=2^k.
Let R be the Boolean algebra of all functions C_N -> F2, with pointwise
product and time shift U. Keep the marked parity E, the ordered adjacent
parent profiles c,d, and an isolated marker q satisfying

    (1+U)q=(1+c)(1+d),    K=(1+E)d(1+c).

The open target is that K=0 forces the whole older prefix to return after
two ticks. The word WHOLE is essential: a selected column can have a
smaller period than the ancestry that drives it.

The proposed resource takes all coefficients of the ancestral profiles,
then closes under time shift and algebraic operations. As the spatial
prefix grows, these generated subobjects form a filtration. An oldest
whole-clock birth gives a unit half-period defect: if j is the oldest
coordinate failing return at N/2, every older half-period defect is zero
and the j-th defect is the constant one. Triangularity proves this without
an independent column-clock assumption.

The question tested here is whether the generated closed subobjects retain
enough information to propagate that defect to the MARKED constraint K.

## Shift-stable Boolean ideals are trivial

Let I be an ideal of the pointwise algebra R with U(I) contained in I.
Since U^N=1, this is full invariance under all positive and negative shifts.
If I contains a nonzero f, choose a time a with f(a)=1. Multiplication by
the point indicator delta_a gives delta_a=f*delta_a in I. Its shifts give
every point indicator. Their sum is the constant one, so I=R.

Thus the only shift-stable Boolean ideals are0 and R. In particular the
ideal generated by a unit oldest defect is already R and carries no
spatial information about where or how later defects are masked. This is
an exact classification, not a finite search or a claim that arbitrary
profile data are genuine ancestry.

## Linear cyclic closure remembers a difference valuation

As a linear representation of U, R is the regular module

    F2[z]/(z^N-1)=F2[z]/((1+z)^N).

A shift-stable linear subspace is an ideal in this cyclic module: powers
of U generate multiplication by every polynomial in z. These ideals are
exactly((1+z)^j), for0<=j<=N. Consequently the linear cyclic span of a
finite tuple of profiles is determined by the MINIMUM ordinary polynomial
valuation among that tuple. Equivalently it records their maximum
binomial degree, not their ordered multiplication relations.

This is distinct from a pointwise Boolean ideal. Confusing the convolution
product of profile polynomials with pointwise multiplication would make
the conclusion unsound. The earlier actual finite-white example180/204
already attains the sharp Hadamard valuation drop3,5 ->1. No stronger
monotone spatial valuation rule is imported here.

## Unital Boolean algebra closure remembers only the joint period

Let f_1,...,f_m be any finite tuple of complete profiles. Let B be the
unital Boolean subalgebra generated by every U^t f_i. Let H be their
common temporal stabilizer:

    h in H iff f_i(s+h)=f_i(s) for every i,s.

H is a subgroup of C_N. Every generator, and hence every element of B,
is constant on cosets of H.

Conversely, two times a,b have identical values on all the generators
exactly when b-a lies in H. Thus the finite list of generator values
separates distinct H-cosets. For a fixed coset, multiply one literal for
each generator: use f where that coset has value one and1+f where it has
value zero. The resulting product is the indicator of precisely that
coset. Therefore B contains every function constant on H-cosets.

So exactly

    B = {v : v(s+h)=v(s) for all h in H}.

For a cyclic group there is one subgroup for each divisor. If p is the
least common JOINT period of the tuple, B is simply the algebra of
p-periodic functions, of dimension p. It is independent of every other
feature of the tuple, including which function is the nearer parent,
the relative phase of the parents, and their marked cover relation.

For all profiles in an actual spatial prefix, the joint period is exactly
the prefix's whole return period. Hence the corresponding spatial algebra
filtration grows precisely at whole-clock births and is unchanged across
plateaus. At the oldest birth of the final whole clock N it is already R.
Retaining all coefficients BEFORE closure does not prevent closure from
discarding their marked association afterward.

This gives a useful exact interpretation of the filtration, but no new
mechanism for the difficult plateaus.

## A literal finite-white check of the lost marked information

The loss of K is visible analytically in the smallest nonconstant genuine
orbit; no state enumeration is needed. Let the oldest bit be constantly
one, and let its single newer bit initially be zero or one. These are the
finite diagonal states2 and3. The actual recurrence gives

    d=1, c=O=1+E for initial state2;
    d=1, c=E     for initial state3.

Both whole words have period two. Their common white-pair mask is zero,
and the isolated marker q=0 satisfies its exact derivative equation.
Both tuples(E,c,d) generate the SAME linear cyclic module and the SAME
unital Boolean algebra, namely the two-dimensional algebra of2-periodic
functions. The corresponding older-prefix clock filtration is also the
same: the constant oldest bit followed by a period-two birth.

But their marked defects differ:

    state2: K=O*1*(1+O)=0;
    state3: K=O*1*(1+E)=O, which is nonzero.

Thus even with the parity function E included in the generated subobject,
the unlabeled closed subobject does not determine K. One must retain the
marked c,d within it, or equivalent joint multiplication data. The example
is entirely finite-white and genuinely coupled. It is NOT a counterexample
to the desired K=0 implication: both older words already return after two.

This check was prespecified as an analytic test of the closure resource,
not a bounded search for a gated high-clock word.

## Exact limit of the conclusion

The classifications above refute using the UNLABELED generated ideal,
cyclic module, or unital algebra/filtration as a closed state that evaluates
and transports the marked cover defect. They do not prove that every
invariant mentioning such objects is useless. In particular they do not
refute a theorem that, within genuine finite-white ancestry, K=0 implies
joint whole period at most two.

Keeping the generators as marked vectors, keeping a tuple module with
its multiplication maps, or retaining the entire ordered cyclic words
avoids this information loss. But no closed spatial induction law for
that richer object has been derived here. The actual relation still needs
to control the lower-coefficient products, their marked parity, and the
unit defect born at the oldest nonreturning whole-prefix coordinate.

The previous phase-cover identity K is therefore still the exact missing
constraint; replacing it by ordinary polynomial factorization or an
unlabeled generated algebra does not solve the boundary-to-head step.
There is no new whole-return, observer, or physical Prize P1 conclusion.
