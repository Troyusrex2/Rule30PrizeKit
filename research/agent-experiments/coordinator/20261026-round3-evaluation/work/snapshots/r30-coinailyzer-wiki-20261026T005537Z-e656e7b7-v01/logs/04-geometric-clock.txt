(1) the parity law.  Golden is two conditions and they split cleanly:
    no `00`  <=>  the first slip is at an even index (phase 0) and every
                  consecutive gap is ODD;
    no `11111` -- a SEPARATE restriction, not characterised here: slips at
                  2,3,4,5 have all-odd gaps and so pass the `00` law, yet the
                  stream carries `11111` and is not Golden.  Only the `00` law
                  is claimed exactly below, and only it is needed.
    65535 slip sets tested; `00` vs the parity law: 0 mismatches
    Golden vs (no 00 and no 11111): 0 mismatches

    and the `11111` clause is NOT a run-length law on the slip set -- three
    candidate thresholds, each tested over the same 65,535 sets:
      run >= 4 : 13008 mismatches
      run >= 5 : 11090 mismatches
      run >= 6 : 11998 mismatches

(3) what survives of the idea
  The clock is the wrong object to put a slip on, but the SLACK side of the
  ScaleCharges statement is untouched by this: floor(k/2) grows with k while a
  single slip contributes one bounded transient, so the slack is not what the
  adversary is fighting.  The fight is over the eventual DENSITY of Theta f,
  which is what `density_floor.py` measures.
