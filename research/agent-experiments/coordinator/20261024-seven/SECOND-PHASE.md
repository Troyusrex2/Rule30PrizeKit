# Further experiments, a new external audit, and the remaining support gap

Problem 1, Q01 and the general coupled question remain open. This follow-up
contains two small checked research modules and several falsified ideas.

## A new independently checked contribution

Gareth Herbert, Claude and GPT submitted
[R30-20261024-8d7751](https://www.wolframcloud.com/obj/rule-30-prize/Agents/status/R30-20261024-8d7751),
which became Lean Verified at 23:45:48 UTC on October 24. It proves absorption
for every right half agreeing with R31 on its first 28 cells, or R495 on its
first 24 cells, including arbitrary later tails. The two examples also have
equal sideways rows without being related by the specified terminal moves.

We read the complete source and independently compiled and replayed all
**106 declarations**, checking eleven headlines with the unchanged official
checker at reference `3eca23c0a9fa6b153313655e6d51835406c2d06e`.
The exact source SHA-256 is
`b0113490bddc1abcc9c9d30b5f6a250108120ddd87de65bd7654d0d42fd89ff5`.
`audit_absorbed_prefixes.py` fetches that exact public source if absent and
refuses different bytes. Evidence is `external/8d7751-evidence/report.json`.
The external theorem and its method are attributed to those authors.

`Rule30DrivenTrapResearch.lean` adds general entry corollaries: any suitable
ether-and-wall entry gives an eventually periodic visible column and hence
an infinitely supported sideways row. Matching either new prefix after an
arbitrary even number of steps is also sufficient. These are conditional
entry criteria; existence of an entry remains an assumption. All **16
declarations and six headlines** passed full replay.

The empty right half was then simulated exactly through **2,000,000 steps**,
keeping its entire growing row. Neither of the two compatible ether phases
entered the known trap. The controls compare 34,176 physical truth-table
cells and reproduce R31 at time four as R495 and its time-nine trap entry.
This bounded absence does not prove nonabsorption or settle CQ01Empty.

A complete four-phase game on a 19-cell prefix has 2,097,152 nodes. Starting
from 218 trap nodes, universal backward closure reaches 8,710 nodes with
entry bound at most 29. The initial empty prefix and its prefix at time
2,000,000 are both outside this set. Its exterior cell is adversarial; a
losing path may be unrealizable by any actual finite right half. The game
result is an obstruction to this particular prefix certificate, not a
counterexample. Independent small-domain fixed-point and physical transition
controls pass. The simulation and game are Python evidence, not Lean proofs.

## Conjugated bit flips

`Rule30GroupPrimitivesResearch.lean` uses the official integer update Phi.
For every natural row `a`, define `g0(a) = a xor 1` and
`g1(a) = a xor (if bit0(a) = bit1(a) then 7 else 3)`. The module proves
`Phi(g0(a)) = g1(Phi(a))`. Both maps are involutions and do not commute.
For `h = g0 composed with g1`, `h^2(a) = a xor 4` and `h^4(a) = a`.
The analogous conjugated flips of the linear Rule 90 update commute.
These are uniform integer identities; they do not prove trace separation.

The module also checks that the official width-six front orbit from `(1,0)`
has least return period 32. This refutes the proposed smaller return bound
16. All **57 declarations and fifteen headlines** passed full kernel replay.

The finite groups generated by conjugated flips through times zero to three
have orders 2, 8, 2^11 and 2^58, with computed derived lengths 1, 2, 3 and 4.
The Rule 90 controls are abelian. Wide-prefix checks agree, but these group
orders are SymPy computations, not Lean results. The time-four Rule 30
calculation hit the resource guard; no result is reported for it. There is
no extrapolation to an unbounded growth or solvability theorem.

## A stronger decoder question, still unresolved

The constructive even-coordinate decoder permits a sharper candidate for
the coupled period-two route: must a finitely supported even-coordinate
input produce free bits containing `00` or `11111`? Actual alternating-center
free bits forbid these words. The stronger decoder assertion is **unproved**.

Simple proposed charge bounds fail on valid golden prefixes:

- Factor one fails at length three: free `010`, even input 1, one black even
  coordinate and two free zeros.
- Factor two fails at length eleven: free `01010110110`, even input 9, two
  black even coordinates and five free zeros.
- Factor three fails at length 42: even input 33595464, five black even
  coordinates and sixteen free zeros. A beam search found it; independent
  recurrence and physical decoder checks confirm the concrete prefix.

Complete enumeration covers all 1,241,152 golden prefixes of length 32;
the minimum even-coordinate black count there is four. Histogram masks are
checked for disjoint coverage and representative counts are independently
recovered. This finite profile does not provide a uniform charge invariant.

A 512-state beam through length 256 found no violation of factor eight.
Its retained length-256 example has 21 black even coordinates and 87 free
zeros; it already refutes factor four. The beam is a heuristic, so its
failure to find a factor-eight counterexample proves nothing. Z3 reports
UNSAT for the factor-eight inequality at length 64 only; length 128 and
the factor-four search at length 64 time out. None is used as a Lean premise.

All 46 primitive rotation classes of periodic golden free words through
period twelve were also tested. Their finite spatial reconstruction cycles
closed, with no finite-even-support candidate. Independent 64-bit decoder
controls check two temporal phases per class. This is another bounded
falsification attempt, not a theorem for arbitrary periods or free streams.

For the empty driven right half, the first 128 even-code coordinates of its
sideways row contain 71 black cells. The test does not infer finite or infinite
support from that prefix. These experiments have not closed the support gap.

## Reproduce

Run `audit_absorbed_prefixes.py`, then `verify_driven_trap.py`, with the same
`--project` and `--lake` options as the earlier replay tools. Run
`verify_group_primitives.py` separately. Every compiler/replay retains the
300-second, 8-GiB, 2-GiB-free guard.

The finite numerical instruments are run with `run_job.py`; their named JSON
files record domains, controls, timeouts and incomplete cases. The SMT script
uses the optional `z3-solver` package; its default local research installation
is under `toolchain/python-research`. No new prize API essay was submitted.
