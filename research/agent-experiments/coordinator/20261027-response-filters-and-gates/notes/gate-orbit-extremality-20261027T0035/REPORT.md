# Extremality assessment

Uniform gate halving remains OPEN. Two exact paper facts survive:

- Every suffix of a whole finite R-orbit maximum/minimum is the maximum/
  minimum of its shorter actual R orbit, for R=D^2.
- If a bit flips while its complete older suffix returns, that bit is1
  at the global maximum and0 at the minimum. The greatest one-step toggle
  is explicitly given by the literal two-step Boolean recurrence.

The missing transfer is the gate language, not extremality. Even the
genuine all-time gated fixed macro row6 has suffix3 whose unshifted gates
fail. The known phase-shift peel repairs that example only because its
head has period two; the variable-head case remains open.

A prespecified stronger shortcut was decisively falsified using only the
existing finite-white plateau868 and its children1736/1737. Child1736's
full phase-orbit maxima are1782 and1342, with gate registers6 and14.
Both pass the local gate pattern, but their first selected gate failures
are at D times3 and4. Thus a nontrivial complete-orbit maximum need not
itself violate the local pattern. This is not a counterexample to the
all-time gate target.
