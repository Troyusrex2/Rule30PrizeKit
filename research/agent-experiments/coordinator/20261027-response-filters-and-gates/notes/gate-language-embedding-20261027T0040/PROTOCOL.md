# Full gate language and a concrete embedding candidate

Words are read low-head-first and padded white beyond their older end.
D(n)=n XOR((n>>1) OR(n>>2)); F=D^2. Let G be the exact even+following-odd
gate language h=1+g, f<=g. Let S=intersection_(j>=0) F^(-j)(G) on finite
words. Trailing older zeros do not change the represented profile.

Candidate embedding, before tests: insert four white cells at position i,
with i>=3 so the gate prefix is unchanged:

    E_i(n)=(n mod2^i)+2^(i+4)*(n>>i).

It preserves all retained spatial indices modulo4. Ask whether this
specific embedding preserves S, or gives a forward simulation for F
under the order generated by four-white-cell insertions. Do not assume
that spatial phase agreement alone supplies a dynamical simulation.

Prespecified analytic control: n=10 has D orbit10,13,10 and is fully
gated. At i=3 the embedded row is130. Verify only its eight displayed
updates130,227,154,245,138,237,146,255,128, where the last even gate fails.
Also compare one macro step: F(10)=10 but F(130)=154. This is one named
counterexample to the embedding, not a sweep or a counterexample to S
being regular.

Audit the exact scope of prior finite-reader, marker, exact-section, and
rank barriers. Separate regularity of each finite preimage language from
regularity or finite convergence of their infinite intersection. Inspect
the already-proved terminal response rewrites before proposing another
equivalence-based embedding. No DFA-size/width sweep or Lean build.
