# Alternating empty-right nonabsorption

Consider literal physical Rule30 on sites p>=1, initially all white, with
prescribed boundary c(t)=1 at even t and c(t)=0 at odd t. Write
u(r)=state(2r+1,1), observing site1 at the white boundary phases.

An existing finite-strip certificate establishes an all-time property of
G(r)=u(146+r): black observations have no distance-two pair, and a black
occurs beyond every bound. If u were eventually period two, a sufficiently
late black G(s) would force G(s+2)=1, contradicting the spacing.

The official ether has seven spatial columns and four temporal phases.
An alignment to the four-slot word [true,false,true,false] must use spatial
column index3 modulo7. Entry into the specified ether-and-wall family ends
its ether prefix at column index2 modulo7, so that prefix has positive
length. The official trap invariant protects physical site1 in an ether
column for every later time. Four physical time steps preserve that column,
which makes u eventually period two, contradicting the previous paragraph.

Thus the empty right half never enters any compatible member of this
ether-and-wall family. For every list of compatible alignments, the official
absorption predicate is false: its universal quantifiers include the empty
right half, finite support bound0, and boundary phase0.

The literal four-entry word matters. The official wordAt function reduces
time modulo4 irrespective of list length, so [true,false] would denote1000,
not alternating forcing.
