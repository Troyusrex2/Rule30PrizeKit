The finite cutoff has true births at4 and10. If the proposed integer-valued potential existed, the birth at4 would imply `E(O5)+1 <= E(O4)`. This is uniform in R and uses actual finite initial rows and their official physical evolution. It does not claim their full rows return, their centers alternate forever, or that they occur as single-seed ages. A rule valid only under an all-future periodic-center premise is also outside the theorem. The result rules out exactly the formal local-observation step inequality, not all approaches to the prize.
