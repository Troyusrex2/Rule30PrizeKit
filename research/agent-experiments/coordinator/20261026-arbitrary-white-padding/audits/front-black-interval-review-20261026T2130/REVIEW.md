## Checked argument

- **Orientation and initial data.** `b` is indexed by spatial front offset first and time second. Its offsets are `i-2,i-1,i`, with the XOR on offset `i-2`. This is the left-front diagonal recurrence, not the older-first finite-word map used by our Safe/height work. Pinned `Rule30FiniteDiagonals.Ld_zero_succ`, `Ld_one_succ`, and `Ld_step` give exactly this recurrence for a full physical configuration white to the left of a chosen anchor. The boundary equations permit either initial bit at offset zero. Thus the claim truly allows arbitrary initial half-line data within this triangular system; it does not assert the recurrence for an arbitrary unanchored full physical row.
- **Uniform onset.** `uniform_period` uses the pinned Boolean transducer lemma with common input onset `2^(i+1)-1` and clock `2^(i+1)`. Adding that clock yields exactly `2^(i+2)-1`. The zero and one boundary coordinates are handled separately. The result is a supplied period, not a least period and not a common clock for the infinite row.
- **Reset and backshift.** A black gate at `A` resets the target independently of its previous state. P-periodic inputs supply the same reset at `A+P`, and determinism propagates equality after `A+1`. The separately assumed positive Q-period at every time from T lets both `t` and `t+P` move forward by `(A+1)*Q` and return to their original values. Both shifted arguments lie past the reset. Thus the proof does not infer backward transient equality from a later reset alone. In the application, Q is the already proved positive power-of-two clock at the common onset.
- **Interval propagation.** For `lo=a+1`, the two starting clocks at offsets `lo-1` and `lo` both divide `P=2^lo`. At the common onset `2^B-1`, black observations at gates `lo` through `B-1` propagate that same period to B. The black value at B is then used for the contradiction. There is no assumed common small clock on the whole row.
- **Erosion and endpoints.** The rule sends `111` to zero, so `[lo+2,B]` is white one tick later. Repeated `000 -> 0` shrinks only the left edge by two offsets each tick. At time `A+P`, offset `B=lo+2P` is still included and is white, contradicting its inherited P-period and initial black value. The interval is closed and contains **2P+1 cells**, not 2P. In particular `a=0` gives `[1,5]`, P=2, and onset31. All inequalities needed for the natural-number subtractions are explicit.

## Meaningful physical-composition caution

The leaf proves an aged white witness in a globally valid free-front recurrence. It does **not** directly prove that a specific witness stays white while traveling toward the driven center. It also does not prove that a partially shielded driven array has the global recurrence or the future clocks required by the argument: `reset_inherits` can use times far beyond the observed block. A valid composition should instantiate the theorem on a genuine free reference evolution and separately transport its witness at the required observation time using a causal-cone agreement theorem. Agreement only on a finite strip is insufficient to invoke the global recurrence theorem directly.

The note explicitly leaves this composition unproved and correctly distinguishes the earlier driven-half marker counterexample. The proposed quantities `lo=20n`, `Bf=lo+2^(lo+1)`, `N=2^Bf-1`, `W=N+9n`, and witness bound `Bf+11n` are not certified by this leaf. The note also correctly retains the further controlled-return/observation hypothesis from the external BlockBound argument. Therefore this result alone is not P1, general periodic-word exclusion, Safe-to-F8 completeness, or a center-observer theorem.
