The public module Rule30EdgeClock imports only the existing FrozenRuler core

Use the actual edge map T: digit0 stays fixed, digit1 XORs digit0, and
digit(j+2) XORs the OR of digits(j+1),j. Codes may have infinite support.

1. Every positive digit j of EVERY black-edge code changes within ceil(j/2)
ticks, from EVERY starting time. The single seed attains that bound. Long
overall clock periods do not imply long consecutive quiet intervals.
Public functions: freeze_below, flip_bound, flip_bound_from, seed_at_bound.

2. If two codes agree below j+1 and differ there, their next digit j+2
differs in every window of ceil(j/2)+1 ticks when j>0. The excluded j=0
case is real: digit1 differences can remain invisible at digit2 forever.
Public functions: higher_excitation, recurrent_excitation.

3. Every such first difference, INCLUDING digit1, escapes the two-digit
band {j+1,j+2} into j+3 or j+4 within ceil(j/2)+2 ticks, recurrently from
every start time. Three consecutive rows of agreement on both interfaces
force the common lower digit j white. The quiet-clock theorem contradicts
a long enough white interval. This is uniform in j and in all infinite
tails, not a larger finite search.
Public functions: three_rows_force_white, two_layer_escape,
recurrent_two_layer_escape. Private prototype: TwoLayerEscape.lean.

Sharpness and an induction barrier
---------------------------------
ClockDelay.lean gives explicit finite same-black-edge parentless witnesses:
i=2n+3; X_n has ones at0,i,i+2; Y_n at0,i+1,i+2.
They first differ at i and agree above K=i+1 through time n+2. Their first
escape occurs EXACTLY at n+3. Thus the new two-layer bound is attained at
infinitely many depths. No escape-time bound depending only on K-i can
hold, even for finite parentless rows. A shared prefix clock must be kept.
Functions: arbitrary_delay, sharp_two_layer, no_distance_only_bound,
common_below, first_difference, parentless, finite_pair.
Checker:67 declarations/6 headlines, no new axioms/warnings.

The construction uses the already-known decoration/edge-shift alias plus
finite propagation from a far common black edge. The known alias itself
is not claimed as new. This differs from TraceAmbiguity's fixed-observer
finite-horizon ambiguity; here the two-layer deadline and its dependence
on the absolute first-difference index are exact.

Why the two-layer proof does not close all widths
-----------------------------------------------
ControlledAlias.lean converts the earlier checked word AAACBCCC barrier to
an explicit every-tick driven example. The two finite starts42,43 differ
forever but agree at EVERY digit>=5 for ALL times. The external controls
are periodic and c is black every16 ticks. They obey the necessary local
ancestor rule h=1 => next c differs from c. The interior is exactly the
edge recurrence, with these two controls substituted below digit0.
Functions: act_is_controlled, ancestor_compatible, controlled_confined,
different_forever, recurrent_black_control;61 declarations/5 headlines.

This is NOT an actual same-black-edge Rule30 counterexample. Those controls
have not been supplied by a genuine finite black-edge ancestry. It proves
that keeping just ONE ancestor compatibility constraint is insufficient.
The source-ancestry requirement cannot be dropped from a proposed extension.
The finite controlled games at widths2,3,4 have no recurrent black-c cycle;
the exact width5 construction shows why those observations cannot be
extrapolated. No larger game census was run.

Remaining exact obligation and stopping point
--------------------------------------------
SameEdgeRigidity is STILL OPEN. It is already kernel-equivalent in the
prior return-action work to AllReturnWordsInjective: two same-length black
prefixes fixed by the same positive return period cannot have the same
actual return section word unless the prefixes agree. The source must be
A^P, not an arbitrary cyclically legal automaton word.

A useful next attempt must either retain enough full source ancestry to
exclude the controlled width5 trap, or provide a nonlocal phase/resource
invariant. Merely extending the two-layer white-clock argument by treating
the inner controls as freely chosen is false. Integer width4 additive
rankings, including two-step versions after parentless reduction, are also
ruled out. Neither dead end should be retried unchanged.

Even proving SameEdgeRigidity would settle only bounded-band confinement;
the moving observation corridor/unbounded-excursion bridge to the center
remains separate. No Problem1 proof, prize claim, formal portal submission,
or claim of Wolfram acceptance is made in this block.
