RESULT
The proposed eventual-S-or-periodic implication is FALSE in the saved width6
relaxation. This is now an all-time Lean theorem, not extrapolation from a scan.
It is NOT a counterexample in the full right half, nor at finite origin.

Two closed19-step paths from window63 have output words
  U = 1011101011110111011
  V = 1010111101110111011.
They admit arbitrary concatenation, preserve no00 and no11111 across joins, and
both contain a complement eight-bit window outside S at the start of each block.
The low-time complement codes are162 and10; neither belongs to
S={8,9,16,18,33,36,66,72,132,144}.

ALLOWING VERSUS NECESSITATING NONPERIODICITY
Concatenation alone does not force nonperiodicity. Constant choices repeat one
word periodically. The precise proved statement is
  EventuallyPeriodic(f_q) IFF EventuallyPeriodic(q).
The choice is read exactly at f_q(19*n+5)=q(n). Taking q to be the indicator of
powers of2 (a previously checked nonperiodic sequence) gives a concrete
nonperiodic output, with bad-S windows arbitrarily far out. Nothing says the
singleton selects this schedule. The encoded freedom is supplied by free outer
input; that is exactly the information discarded by this finite-strip relaxation.
