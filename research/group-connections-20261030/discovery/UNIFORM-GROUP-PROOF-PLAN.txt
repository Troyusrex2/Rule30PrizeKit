Research proof plan, 2026-10-30 01:24UTC. Not yet a verified theorem.

Stronger than initial identity-vacuum idea: ANY group-valued local Lax
cocycle for Rule30 with L on w bits and M on w+1 bits appears gauge-equivalent
to a CONSTANT background a=L(zeros). No vacuum=identity restriction needed.

Equation: L(image input)*M(prefix input)=M(suffix input)*L(central input),
input widthw+2, Rule30 image widthw. Multiplication is noncommutative.

1. Normalize L(0,rest)=a via a vertex gauge g on words of lengthw-1.
Let P(v)=0 ++ init(v). P^(w-1)v=zeros. Define
G_0(v)=1; G_(k+1)(v)=a*G_k(Pv)*L(0++v)^-1.
G_(k+1)(v)=G_k(v) if P^k(v)=zeros, by inductionk; atzero the factor is a*a^-1.
Thus g(v)=G_(w-1)(v) satisfies g(v)=a*g(Pv)*L(0++v)^-1.
The edge gauge Lg(word)=g(tailword)*L(word)*g(initword)^-1 is anchored at a.
The temporal gauge Mg(u)=g(image u)*M(u)*g(central u)^-1 retains rangew+1.
Local Rule30 images commute with prefix/tail cropping, so the Lax equation
is gauge-invariant by associativity and inverse cancellation.

2. An anchored L of width>=2 must forget its last bit. Every interior output
word is realizable with the TWO rightmost input registers white, by solving
leftwards. The two outer input bits then independently toggle the two output
endpoints. The Lax equation makes L(output endpoints) factor as B(right)*A(left).
Both outputs with first bit0 equal a, so B(false)=B(true) by cancellation;
therefore both outputs with first bit1 also agree. All middle words arbitrary.

3. Once L forgets its last bit, comparing the equation with a final input0/1
forces M to forget its last bit too. Strip both by one cell; repeat induction.

4. Width1 base, NO anchoring assumption needed. Put a=L0,b=L1,m=M00,
p=M01,n=M10,q=M11. The Rule30 truth-table equations give:
000: a*m=m*a
001: b*m=p*a
010: b*p=n*b
011: b*p=q*b -> n=q by right cancellation
101: a*n=p*a
110: a*q=n*b -> a*n=n*b
Hence b*p=n*b=a*n=p*a=b*m, so p=m by left cancellation.
Then b*m=m*a=a*m, so b=a by right cancellation. No finiteness/commutativity.

5. Therefore anchored L is constant a at every width. Undo gauge:
L(word)=g(tailword)^-1 * a * g(initword).
On any cyclic spatial row of lengthN the right-to-left L product is conjugate
to a^N, independent of the row. A putative invariant has no configuration data.

SCOPE: L finite width, M one-cell halo, homogeneous translation covariance,
GROUP-valued (invertible/cancellative). Noninvertible semigroups, nonlocal or
unbounded temporal transport, or other observables are not ruled out. Larger

extension is NOT proved here. This is not a seed aperiodicity theorem.
