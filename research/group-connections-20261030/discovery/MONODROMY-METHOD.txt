Local matrix-product experiment, 2026-10-30.

For a word of w adjacent row bits, assign L(word) in a finite group. For a
word of w+1 bits, assign temporal M(word). An input window a0..a(w+1) gives
old central block a1..aw and new block F(a0,a1,a2)..F(a(w-1),aw,a(w+1)).
The equation is L_new M_prefix = M_suffix L_old.

On a cyclic row, products of L from right to left then evolve by conjugacy:
P_new=M_root P_old M_root^-1. With L(vacuum)=I this also applies to finite
defects padded by zeros. Gauge at the spatial vertices (words of lengthw-1)
acts L_edge -> G_target L_edge G_source^-1, preserving the local class and
the permitted temporal window. An undirected spanning tree of deBruijn
vertices can therefore have all L labels set to I. The allones loop must
also have L=I because its updated block is allzero and temporal transport
is invertible. A remaining nonidentity edge is a nongauge L assignment.

For each L assignment, solve for M without enumerating every M vertex.
A directed BFS of temporal deBruijn vertices (lengthw+1) gives
M_v=A_v M_root B_v with A_root=B_root=I. A tree edge e:u->v gives
A_v=L_new(e) A_u, B_v=B_u L_old(e)^-1. For any edge, consistency requires
U M_root = M_root V where
U=A_v^-1 L_new(e) A_u, V=B_v L_old(e) B_u^-1.
Precompute a bitset of allowed M_root for every(U,V), intersect it over all
non-tree edges, and reject when empty. Recover one root if any survive,
then independently check every original local equation.

This is a finite-group cocycle search, not a statistical invariant fit and
not an assumption of a closed coarse row. A positive law would need a new
argument connecting its spatial product to the actual singleton boundary.
No such bridge is presumed. A negative result is restricted to the registered
groups/windows/vacuum normalization.
