Audit of the incoming Hankel, 2-kernel, dynamics, and homology proposals
Troy Lowry / OpenAI Codex, 2026-10-30. Methodological feedback, not a prize proof.

1. Fixed-window kernel counts cannot diverge.
   A vector with M Boolean coordinates has only2^M possibilities; the displayed
   t=0..M convention instead has M+1 coordinates and bound2^(M+1). A correct
   infinite-kernel argument needs growing windows, or infinitely many actual
   infinite subsequences distinguished by proved witnesses. Finite growth does
   not establish nonautomaticity; a finite plateau does not establish automaticity.
   Every finite observed prefix has an eventually-zero, hence automatic and
   eventually-periodic extension with exactly the same observations.

2. The bound |K_2|<=P is not valid for every purely periodic sequence.
   For the7-periodic sequence1 at residues0,1 and0 elsewhere, decimation slopes
   1,2,4 modulo7 each give7 distinct phases, for21 distinct kernel elements.
   An odd period P gives the elementary upper bound P*ord_P(2), not P in general;
   a transient needs further treatment. Finiteness of the kernel remains valid.
   Over F2, infinite cardinality and infinite linear-span dimension are equivalent,
   but proving nonautomaticity is stronger than the prize's nonperiodicity target.

3. Finite Hankel/Berlekamp-Massey measurements do not prove the missing bridge.
   Eventual periodicity implies bounded rank. A uniform asymptotic lower bound
   would suffice, but high ranks at4096/16384 are not a rank-production lemma.
   Conversely, a low finite rank does not refute eventual unbounded growth unless
   a concrete all-N bound and constants were part of the claim. A reset merges
   predecessor states; it does not by itself inject an independent linear
   constraint into the true center's Hankel matrix. Existing checked periodic
   countermodels already separate generic reset occurrence from center rank.

4. Ordinary Boolean differentials collapse.
   If the actual phase algebra imposes x^2=x over F2, any ordinary derivation D
   obeying Leibniz has D(x)=D(x^2)=xD(x)+xD(x)=0. Thus its ordinary Kahler
   differentials vanish. Working in the unreduced polynomial ring avoids this
   algebraic collapse but requires proving that the proposed forms descend to
   physical Boolean configurations. Discrete cubical cochains/differences are
   different constructions and must be defined; they do not automatically have
   the ordinary derivation laws. This is a standard algebraic observation, not
   a new Rule30 theorem.

5. Expansion and measure decay need exact quantifiers.
   A periodic observable can be a factor of an odometer or an expanding system.
   A positive Lyapunov exponent or many independent driving bits does not prove
   that this particular center readout retains them. Likewise, survival
   probability2^-n can coexist with one infinite surviving branch. An absolute
   live-count bound below1, or a rank decreasing on every surviving branch, is
   materially stronger. W<=8 counterexamples to a windowed bound do not rule
   out existence of some larger fixed W.

6. What the current formal work actually supplies.
   PR58 classifies the displayed local group-valued flat connections for all
   widths and groups, including noncommutative groups and nonidentity vacuum.
   Singular semigroup observables and genuinely nonlocal constructions remain
   outside it. A separately checked13-state example shows why profile decoding
   must retain finite white ancestry: equal nonalternating final profiles can
   have different infinite pasts when that assumption is omitted. Neither result
   proves center aperiodicity. An argument using a global invariant must still
   establish how finite ancestry selects the needed branch or prevents masking.
