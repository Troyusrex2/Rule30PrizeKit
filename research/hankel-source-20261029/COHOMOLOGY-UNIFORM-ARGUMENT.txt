TARGET

For Rule30 on binary configurations, every translation-invariant finite-range
additive conserved density over F2 is a constant plus a spatial coboundary.
Equivalently, there is no nontrivial charge of this local additive kind. This
would be a barrier to one proposed homology route, NOT a proof of Problem1.

1. NORMAL FORM MODULO COBBOUNDARIES

Write a local Boolean function rho in its unique squarefree algebraic normal
form (ANF) over F2. Identify monomials that differ only by a spatial shift:
sum_i monomial(x shifted by i) is unchanged by translating its support.
Move the minimum index of every nonconstant monomial to 0 and XOR equal
shapes. Constants are a separate, trivial conserved density.

This is a canonical representative of the local additive charge. The
difference from the original density is a finite sum of adjacent spatial
differences, hence a coboundary. Every nonconstant monomial in the resulting
representative contains x_0; thus rho(0, rest)=0. If the representative is
nonzero, let m be its largest monomial span (max index - min index + 1).
For m>=2, the mixed derivative in x_0 and x_(m-1) is a nonzero function Q of
the interior m-2 bits. For m=1 the only nonzero representative is x_0.

This is also valid over cyclic words of every sufficiently large length:
take a ring length greater than twice the maximal support width. Each
nonempty monomial shape then has a unique cyclic gap longer than half the
ring, so different anchored shapes are distinguishable. Thus a nonzero
canonical coefficient cannot disappear just by summing over translations.

2. EXTREMAL SPAN UNDER THE RULE30 PULLBACK

Let F_i=x_(i-1)+x_i+x_(i+1)+x_i*x_(i+1), over F2.
For m>=2, compose rho with (F_0,...,F_(m-1)). This polynomial uses positions
-1,...,m, so its maximum possible span is m+2. Its mixed derivative in the
two OUTER input variables x_(-1),x_m is exactly

  (1+x_(m-1)) * Q(F_1,...,F_(m-2)).

Reason: x_(-1) occurs only in F_0 with coefficient 1; x_m occurs only in
F_(m-1), with coefficient 1+x_(m-1). Boolean derivatives are finite
differences, not Kahler differentials in the Boolean quotient.

This expression is NOT identically zero. Set x_(m-1)=0. Fix x_(m-2)=0 too.
For ANY desired word (F_1,...,F_(m-2)), solve backwards using left
permutivity: x_(i-1)=F_i + (x_i OR x_(i+1)). Thus choose an interior word
where Q=1, and the displayed derivative is 1. The same decoding and
endpoint-rectangle assertion are checked in EndpointTransport.lean.

Therefore the composed density has at least one nonzero ANF monomial with
both outer endpoints -1 and m. Every such monomial has span m+2 and those
are the only possible placements of that span in [-1,m]. Consequently
canonicalization by spatial shifts cannot cancel these coefficients: two
different maximal-span monomials here cannot be translates of one another.
Shorter original monomials cannot reach both outer endpoints either.

Thus the canonical pullback has span EXACTLY m+2. For m=1, F_0 modulo shifts
is x_0+x_0*x_1, whose span is 2. In both cases the span strictly increases.

3. CONSEQUENCE AND LIMITS

An invariant additive density would have the same canonical class before
and after pullback, contradicting strict span increase. Iteration would also
exclude any finite cycle of these classes (including a moving spatial
frame, since the quotient already discards translations).

This does NOT exclude arbitrary invariants. The assumptions are finite
spatial range, additive sum of translates, F2-valued charge, and validity for
ALL configurations. Seed-specific, nonlocal, boundary-relative,
inhomogeneous, or nonadditive constructions are outside this statement.
It supplies no implication from bounded center Hankel rank to such a charge.

There is a stronger checked warning about that missing bridge. The endpoint
decoder/rectangle property holds for EVERY rule left XOR g(center,right)
with g(false,r)=r. In particular it holds for Rule210, g(c,r)=NOT c AND r.
The official kit proves Rule210's single-seed center is white for every
positive time. EndpointCountermodel.lean combines these statements into an
all-width, all-time countermodel to endpoint-control implying seed
aperiodicity. This reuses the official center210 theorem, with credit.
The span-growth paper argument also applies to Rule210: the two white
right registers still make the outer right variable permutive, and its
single-site quotient is x_0*x_1. Thus even a completed no-local-charge
theorem would not distinguish Rule30 from this periodic-center control.
Any prize route using it must add a genuinely seed-specific step.
