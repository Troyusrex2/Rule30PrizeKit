Main constructive result: uniform four-layer escape
--------------------------------------------------
PR61 https://github.com/WolframInstitute/Rule30PrizeKit/pull/61
Head17098426f002c3cfc585b2b754879d93a75efacc adds the companion public core
module Rule30DefectClockBudget; Rule30EdgeClock retains the sharp clock and
two-layer results. The new module's47 declarations and5 headlines replayed
through the unchanged checker, with no new axioms or proof-source warnings.

For EVERY pair of infinite codes x,y with a common black edge, agreeing
below j+1 and differing there, a difference appears at j+5 or j+6 within
7*(ceil(j/2)+1) ticks, from EVERY starting time. No support, period, or upper
tail assumption is used. The four-layer bound is not claimed optimal.
Public functions: measure_step, four_layer_escape,
recurrent_four_layer_escape.

Mechanism: an explicit Boolean rank on the relative band and its lower
clock is at most6. During confinement it never increases, and every black
tick of the shared lower clock costs at least1. The all-depth quiet-clock
bound supplies a black tick in every ceil(j/2)+1 window. Seven windows
cannot be paid. The transition inequality is independently kernel checked
over ALL Boolean assignments; the absolute source depth and time remain
unbounded. This is not an extension of the finite absolute-band census.

The rank is neither additive nor strictly decreasing every tick. It may
stall while the lower clock is white. This explains why the earlier local
one-/two-step ranking obstructions do not apply to it. It is generated by
an exact finite dynamic program for black exposure, compressed to a Boolean
decision tree, and then checked independently in Lean.

The local method's exact width limit
-----------------------------------
ControlledBudget.lean proves a stronger qualitative statement: under the
explicit interior recurrence U(c,h), no four-digit trapped defect can
coexist with recurrent black c and the necessary compatibility
  h(t)=1 => c(t+1) != c(t).
The recurrence frequency need not have any uniform bound or period.
Functions: budget_step, recurrent_rank_impossible, no_four_digit_trap.

Width5 DOES admit a forever-trapped defect in this relaxed model, even
when TWO genuine lower recurrences are imposed:
  c(t+1) = c(t) XOR (h(t) OR g(t)),
  h(t+1) = h(t) XOR (g(t) OR f(t)).
The explicit finite starts106 and113 differ forever at digit0, yet agree
at EVERY digit>=5 for ALL times. c is black recurrently. The input period
is8; the inner prefix period is16. The chronological categorical input is
CBCABCCB, or BCCBACBC in the reverse word convention.

TwoClockAlias.lean checks the full sixteen-phase invariant and all spatial
indices, not a finite-time simulation. It also checks the two lower
recurrences, compatibility, recurrent black input, prefix swapping after
8 updates, and the square-word section collision. ControlledBudget's
five_digit_recurrent_trap assembles these into a single explicit witness.
Thus5 is the minimum trapped-band width in the relaxed controlled model.

This is NOT a pair with genuine finite black-edge ancestors. The two
inner starts have different digit0; the same external controls stand in
for a common earlier prefix. The result does not refute SameEdgeRigidity.
It proves why two lower recurrence constraints cannot replace the full
anchored source. No natural-number budget can pay every recurrent black
tick on this example, irrespective of how that budget is represented.

A stronger ancestry filter rejects the OLD trap uniformly
--------------------------------------------------------
TemporalAncestry.lean proves that no actual categorical boundary memory
history has chronological factor ACC. A white adjacent pair whose leading
cell rises black must have that cell white one tick later. This holds at
all depths, edge values, times and supports.

Consequently an actual return word for a returning prefix cannot begin A
and end CC. In particular EVERY positive repeated power of AAACBCCC is
excluded for ALL prefix lengths and periods8m. The previous finite P8
closure table is unnecessary for this entire family.
Functions: no_ACC_from, word_end_obstruction, all_bad_powers_unreachable.

The new BCCBACBC example respects ACC exclusion and both lower recurrences;
it is a stronger obstruction to relaxed ancestry arguments. Do not keep
using AAACBCCC as though it were compatible with two full ancestor levels.

Exact boundary transfer and relation to the target
-------------------------------------------------
WordBoundary.lean sharpens the earlier actual-return-word bridge:
if two prefixes return after P and their actual A^P section words agree,
then giving them ANY common infinite tail makes their evolving tails agree
at ALL ticks from the exact cut n onward. No 2P band enlargement is needed.
Equal sampled return FUNCTIONS alone were the weaker information that
required the older enlargement. Functions: memory_equal_all,
shared_words_confine_at_cut. This does not assert that the words are
injective; their injectivity remains the missing claim.

MemorySeparation.lean adds the fully visible leading memory bit at n-1.
Combining this with four-layer escape, every genuine source-word collision
whose first difference is at i>0 inside the prefix satisfies i+6<=n.
In other words at least five unresolved defect digits must lie below the
last fully observed memory bit. This is an all-depth source constraint,
not full injectivity. Function: source_collision_separation.

PrefixPeriodReflection is explicitly defined in WordBoundary.lean and
remains a proposition, not a theorem. Full AllReturnWordsInjective would
imply it (reflection_of_word_injective). No proof of either was found.
